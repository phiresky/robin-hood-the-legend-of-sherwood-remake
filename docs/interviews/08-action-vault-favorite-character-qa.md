Returning to England from the Crusades, King Richard the Lionheart has been taken prisoner. Held in an Austrian castle, his chances of escape or rescue are virtually non-existent. Of course, it will be possible to gain his freedom by paying a ransom, but the amount demanded by his captors is exorbitant. Neither is the regent, Prince John, eager to pay it. In his brother's absence, he has grown very accustomed to the power of the throne, and together with his supporters, a number of powerful Norman barons, he is actively lining his pockets through oppressive taxation, the proclamation of unjust laws and various other thinly veiled unscrupulous activities. The people have little reason for hope, but deep in the heart of Sherwood Forest north of Nottingham, one man and his followers refuse to bend and continue to live free.

Now late in development at Spellbound Studios, [Robin Hood - The Legend of Sherwood](http://www.robinhood-game.com/) will allow you to assume the role of the legendary hero, leading Little John, Friar Tuck, other members of the Merry Men band and Lady Marian in a fight for the common people, against the seemingly overwhelming forces of the tyrant. Described as a game of tactics and action, it will take the protagonists on an adventure through locations ranging from the famed forest to Nottingham Castle, York et al armed with bows and arrows, swords and their own steely resolve. Recently, we were pleased to receive the opportunity to learn a lot more about it in an extensive interview. In this Robin Hood - The Legend of Sherwood Q&A; #2, we meet the player characters and then nine team members from the developer, European publisher Wanadoo and North American counterpart Strategy First tell us their favorites.

* * *

**Robin And His Merry Men**

**Robin Hood**
Our hero, Robin of Locksley, has just returned from the Crusades where he fought beside the valiant King Richard the Lion-Hearted. However, he discovers that the King has not yet returned; the Kingdom is in the hands of the King's brother, Prince John, and it would seem that he is incapable of maintaining order. Indeed, when Robin's father died while he was away, the Sheriff of Nottingham took over Robin's land by claiming that he was dead too!

The Sheriff is also terrorizing the local population by imposing heavy taxes on poor villagers. Robin decides to reclaim his rightful due and free the people from the tyrannical Sheriff by defeating him. But the Sheriff is only a pawn...

**Little John**
Big and strong, Little John is a real force of nature. A woodcutter from a small village on the edge of Sherwood Forest, he wants to join Robin's group but hasn't yet had the chance to meet him...

**Maid Marian**
This charming young lady is a childhood friend of Robin's... He is still in love with her, but hasn't heard from her in a long time. What will she think when she learns that Robin has become a brigand?

**Will Scarlet**
One of Robin's nephews, Will Scarlet has a bad reputation: he is said to be brutal and violent. Scarlet loves fighting and is always looking for trouble... some say that he dresses in red so that nobody will notice the blood stains on his clothes...

**Friar Tuck**
This portly monk is Maid Marian's confessor. Although his first encounter with Robin was somewhat... brutal, he eventually decided to work for him as a spy in return for the occasional feast...

**Stuteley**
This grumpy craftsman is an old friend of Robin's family and an enemy of the Sheriff. Shrewd and good with his hands, Stuteley is skilled at building traps that are useful in attacking the Sheriff's convoys. Stuteley is well organized and far-sighted; it was he who set up the secret headquarters in Sherwood.

**Action Vault: Among the title character, the various playable Merry Men and Maid Marian, which one is your personal favorite, and why?**

**Anne Devouassoux
Producer, Wanadoo**
My favorite character is Lady Marian. Apparently she's nice and sweet, she can cure wounded people, but she's also a fierce archery killer.

**Jean-Marc Haessig
Creative Director, Spellbound Studios**
My favorite character is Will because he's very fun to play with. Will's favorite weapon is a flail, and as soon as an enemy is stunned, he can't help knocking him down again.

**Aline Gugliermina
Product Manager, Wanadoo**
Will, because he can climb the walls, and it's always very useful in life :)

**Yohann Steinberg
Lead Tester, Wanadoo**
Little John, as he's tall and can knock people out. He doesn't kill them, but avoids them to annoy his Merry Men.

**Didier Schwaller
Tester, Wanadoo**
Little John, because he's powerful. When he's surrounded by a dozen enemies, I really like to have him make a simple turn around with his stick, knocking them all down at once!

**Tiphaine Locqueneux
Communication Manager, Wanadoo**
Maid Marian of course, because she 's in love with Robin but nobody knows. :)

**Susannah Skerl
Producer, Strategy First**
I like Robin best because he's an archery whiz, and his game animations remind me of Errol Flynn movies from the '30s! Robin is also one of the most versatile characters and has a wide array of actions available to him - very fun to play.

**Pro Sotos
Product Manager, Strategy First**
My favorite is Little John. Not only is he very strong, but he also has the coolest move. When he gets into a fight, if you move your cursor in a figure eight, he will stick his staff into the ground, do a spin around it and knock out everyone in his path.

**Fiona Costello
Product Manager, Strategy First**
I like Friar Tuck, he's fat and cuddly and uses beer as a weapon.

**Using beer as a weapon seems like a rather unfortunate waste, but apparently, no sacrifice is too great when tyranny must be overthrown. Fortunately, the other player characters are able to use a variety of other combat stratagems, so perhaps there will be some left over with which to celebrate victories. In any event, we appreciate the various team members telling us about the characters and their favorites, and we look forward to seeing them in action soon.**
